Privacy Policy

TV Shows Quiz Premium

Effective Date: March 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TV Shows Quiz Premium ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: TV Shows Quiz Premium is a paid app with no advertisements. We collect minimal data only to provide leaderboard functionality through Google Play Games Services. We do not sell your data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you use Google Play Games Services features (leaderboards), the following information may be collected:

  • Google Play Games Player ID: A unique identifier assigned by Google Play Games Services
  • Game Activity: Your quiz scores submitted to leaderboards
  • Display Name: Your Google Play Games profile name (as configured in your Google account)

1.2 Information Stored Locally

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted:

  • Quiz statistics (questions answered, correct answers, time played)
  • Achievement progress
  • App preferences and settings
  • Category completion status

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Leaderboard Functionality: Displaying your scores on global leaderboards
  • Game Features: Tracking achievements and progress within Google Play Games

We do NOT use your information for:

  • Advertising or marketing
  • Selling to third parties
  • User profiling or analytics
  • Tracking across other apps

3. Data Sharing

Your data is shared only in the following circumstances:

  • Public Leaderboards: Your display name and scores are visible to other players on Google Play Games leaderboards
  • Google Play Games Services: Data is processed by Google according to their Privacy Policy

We do not share, sell, or transfer your personal information to any other third parties.

4. Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • All data transmitted to Google Play Games Services is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Local data is stored in Android's secure app storage
  • We do not store any data on our own servers

5. Your Choices and Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Opt-out of Leaderboards: You can use the app without signing into Google Play Games. All features except leaderboards will work normally.
  • Delete Local Data: Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Delete Google Play Games Data: Visit Google Play Settings to manage or delete your Play Games data
  • Manage Google Account: Control your data through your Google Account Privacy Settings

6. Children's Privacy

TV Shows Quiz Premium is a general audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains quiz questions about TV shows, some of which may be rated for mature audiences. Parental guidance is recommended.

Google Play Games Services has its own age restrictions and parental controls as described in Google's Family Policy.

7. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party service:

  • Google Play Games Services: For leaderboards and achievements. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics services, or other third-party data collection tools.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

We recommend reviewing this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

9. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained until you uninstall the app
  • Google Play Games Data: Retained according to Google's data retention policies until you delete it

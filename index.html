Privacy Policy

TV Shows Quiz

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TV Shows Quiz ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: TV Shows Quiz is a free app supported by advertising. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to show ads. Where required (EEA, UK), we ask for your consent first. Leaderboards use Google Play Games Services. We do not sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you use Google Play Games Services features (leaderboards), the following information may be collected:

  • Google Play Games Player ID: A unique identifier assigned by Google Play Games Services
  • Game Activity: Your quiz scores submitted to leaderboards
  • Display Name: Your Google Play Games profile name (as configured in your Google account)

1.2 Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner and interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob unless you purchase the optional ad-removal product. Google and its partners may collect and process the following through the AdMob SDK:

  • Advertising ID (resettable by you in your device settings)
  • IP address, device and OS information, app version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and approximate location derived from the IP address

This data is used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, if you consent, to personalise ads. See how Google uses data when you use partner apps.

1.3 Purchase Information

If you buy the optional ad-removal product, Google Play processes the transaction. We do not receive or store your payment card or other payment details. The app stores your ad-free entitlement locally and checks your existing purchase through Google Play to restore it.

1.4 Information Stored Locally

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted:

  • Quiz statistics (questions answered, correct answers, time played)
  • Achievement progress
  • App preferences and settings
  • Category completion status
  • Your ad-free entitlement

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Leaderboard Functionality: Displaying your scores on global leaderboards
  • Game Features: Tracking achievements and progress within Google Play Games
  • Advertising: Showing ads through Google AdMob to fund the free app
  • Purchase Restoration: Restoring the optional ad-removal product through Google Play

We do NOT use your information for:

  • Selling your personal information
  • Our own user profiling or analytics

3. Data Sharing

Your data is shared only in the following circumstances:

  • Public Leaderboards: Your display name and scores are visible to other players on Google Play Games leaderboards
  • Google Play Games Services: Data is processed by Google according to their Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Advertising data is processed by Google and its ad partners

We do not sell your personal information or share it with other third parties.

4. Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • All data transmitted to Google Play Games Services is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Local data is stored in Android's secure app storage
  • We do not store any data on our own servers

5. Your Choices and Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Opt-out of Leaderboards: You can use the app without signing into Google Play Games. All features except leaderboards will work normally.
  • Ad Consent and Personalisation: In the EEA/UK you can change your ad consent at any time under Settings → "Ad privacy settings". On Android, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Remove Ads: You can purchase the optional ad-removal product. Deleting local app data does not cancel or refund the purchase; Google Play restores eligible purchases.
  • Delete Local Data: Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.
  • Delete Google Play Games Data: Visit Google Play Settings to manage or delete your Play Games data.
  • Manage Google Account: Control your data through your Google Account Privacy Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

TV Shows Quiz is a general audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains quiz questions about TV shows, some of which may be rated for mature audiences. Parental guidance is recommended.

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly serve ads to them. Google Play Games Services has its own age restrictions and parental controls as described in Google's Family Policy.

7. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Games Services: For leaderboards and achievements. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): For advertising. AdMob policies and data use
  • Google Play Billing: For the optional one-time in-app purchase that removes ads. Payments and purchase records are handled by Google Play; we do not receive your payment details.

We do not use any other analytics services or third-party data collection tools.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

We recommend reviewing this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

9. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained until you uninstall the app
  • Google Play Games Data: Retained according to Google's data retention policies until you delete it
  • Advertising and Purchase Data: Retained by Google and its partners according to their applicable policies
